## Rules used

Because the ATC '27 call is not posted, formatting and anonymity follow the posted **ATC '25** submission instructions, which are the ATC '25/'26 rules named for this draft:

`https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc25/submission-instructions`

- Full papers: **at most 12 pages** including text, figures, tables, and footnotes. **References are extra** and are not set in a smaller font.
- US letter. Text block 7 in × 9 in, two columns, 0.33 in gutter. 10-point type on 12-point leading.
- Pages numbered. In-document references hyperlinked.
- **Double-blind.** No author or institution names. No “reference removed for blind review.” Own prior work, if cited, is cited as third-party work. No repository URL and no cluster or university name that identifies the authors. Timing hosts are “a university cluster” with CPU model AMD EPYC 7542.
- Template: official `usenix2019_v3.sty`, downloaded from `https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/usenix2019_v3.sty`, linked from `https://www.usenix.org/conferences/author-resources/paper-templates` (sample `usenix2019_v3.2.tex` on the same page).
