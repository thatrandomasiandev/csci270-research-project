## What ran

The gate sits in `acts/reference_run.py` immediately after the entry diff and before discovery or replay. The changed fraction is the share of entries whose content hash is not already covered. A cold cache is a fill and is not gated. If the fraction is strictly above the threshold, TARE runs the stock argv on the whole reference, writes that output, and records

`fallback: f_delta=.. > threshold=..`

The decision is `FALLBACK`. The threshold is `--f-threshold` when that flag is set. Otherwise it is `(1-c)/a` from the cache table `cost_history` when that fit gives a positive break-even (`a > 0` and `c < 1`). Otherwise it is the documented default `0.80`, from the laptop fit `a=1.06`, `c=0.152`.
